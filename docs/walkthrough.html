Start of day

One video unlocks the day

Every account starts at 0 credits. The first video of the day unlocks the community floor: 16 credits, about an hour of Haiku, the same in every country. After that, each extra video adds 4.

The unlock card sits above the composer until the floor is unlockedThe video plays in place, with its own progress bar and the reward shown
Channel · Chat

Chat

Haiku 5.5 is the default at 1 credit a message. Smart pick suggests Sonnet, Opus or Fable only when a task needs it. Every reply shows its model and cost.

While the answer is written: a working card with "Watch 20s +4"After: one sponsored line under the answer, never inside it
Channel · Code

Code

Works on a connected GitHub repo: reads the project, plans, edits, runs tests and opens a pull request. It never pushes to main. Long builds get a cost check before they start.

A developer-tool sponsor in the working card, such as a hosting previewThe right panel stays up through the whole build
Channel · Design

Design

Describe a screen or page and get a live mock you can keep refining. Pages run in a locked preview with version history.

A 320×50 banner under the result, rounded to match the cards
Channel · Images

Images

Four takes on every prompt, at a flat 3 credits. This channel stays Coming soon at launch until an image provider is added, since Claude doesn't make images.

Print and merch sponsors fit naturally here
Projects

Projects

Each project keeps its chats, files, instructions, connected repo, coworkers and teammates together. Instructions apply to every chat and coworker inside it, and the project shows what it cost this week.

Both panels run here too: sidebar card and right panel
Coworkers

Coworkers

AI teammates that work in the background on a schedule, each inside a project, each with its own daily credit cap. They ask before anything leaves Wanlly: a pull request, an SMS through your Twilio, a post. Unlocks at 7 days old.

The approval list comes first, so nothing waits on you unseen
Profile

Profile and settings

One page for everything: name, student check, default model, theme, trust level, ad topics, where extra videos go, connections, notifications, devices and your data. Country comes from the network and can't be edited, because it sets ad rates.

People choose the topics their ads are about, which also makes them pay better
Earning

Earn credits

Open from the credits pill, the sidebar or the right panel. Videos, sponsor trials, surveys once the account is a week old, a streak, and "Watch to fund a creator", which sends the video's value to the community pool.

Every way to earn is opt-in and labelled
Phone

On a phone

Same channels, one column. The sidebar opens from the menu with its sponsor card, and a 320×50 banner sits in a tray under the page.

Never over the composer, never inside an answer
Dark mode

Dark mode

Follows the system setting, or the choice in Profile. Sponsor covers keep their own colours and sit in the same rounded frames.

Sponsors shown (Lumen Laptops, Courseway, Internly, Fieldnote, Northbeam DB, Railhouse, Typecase, Printwell) are made up for the design. Real ones come from the ad partners in the plan.
