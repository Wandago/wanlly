Why video prices drop, and how to slow it

Each advertiser caps how often one person sees their video. With one network, the same few advertisers run out fast and later videos pay less. With several networks bidding, there are more advertisers to rotate through. Rewards always follow what each video actually paid, so a cheaper video gives fewer credits but never loses money.
