    What keeps it balanced

    Free is an allowance, not unlimitedEveryone gets daily credits. Past that, people watch a spot, upgrade, or wait until tomorrow.
    Rewards follow the payoutA video spot gives back about 70% of what it earned. Turn the switch off in a lower-rate region to see the loss.
    Heavy users move to Plus or their own keyNo ad volume pays for building an operating system. Their usage goes through Plus or their own API key.
    Sponsors stay beside the workNothing ever appears inside an answer, and nothing blocks a finished result.
