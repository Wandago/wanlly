1

The whole system

People reach Wanlly through Cloudflare, which filters bots before anything else runs. The app checks who they are, what they can afford, and routes their request to the right AI. Every credit in and out is written to one ledger.

2

One message, start to finish

Before any AI runs, Wanlly checks the person's limits, estimates the cost and sets aside the maximum it could cost. When the answer finishes, it charges what was actually used and returns the rest.

sequenceDiagram
  participant P as Person
  participant E as Cloudflare
  participant A as Wanlly app
  participant L as Limits and ledger
  participant S as Smart pick
  participant M as Claude
  participant D as Ad slot
  P->>E: Sends a message
  E->>A: Passes bot and rate checks
  A->>L: Window, weekly cap, global safety cap OK?
  A->>S: Which model fits? (tiny Haiku call)
  S-->>P: Suggests a model, person confirms
  A->>L: Reserve the maximum this could cost
  A->>D: Show the sponsor card while working
  A->>M: Send the request (cached history)
  M-->>P: Answer streams in
  M-->>A: Real token count
  A->>L: Charge real cost, release the rest
  A->>D: Fold sponsor card to one line

Why reserve first

  • A request can't start unless the balance covers its worst case.
  • Nobody can overspend, even with several tabs open at once.

Adjust here

  • Whether smart pick asks every time or only when it suggests a bigger model.
  • How many credits each model costs.
3

Earning credits

Credits only appear after the ad network's own server confirms a video was watched. The browser never adds credits, so they can't be faked.

sequenceDiagram
  participant P as Person
  participant A as Wanlly app
  participant N as Ad network
  participant R as Risk scorer
  participant L as Ledger
  P->>A: Taps Watch 20s
  A->>R: Is this account allowed to earn now?
  R-->>A: Yes (caps, cooldown, risk OK)
  A->>N: Request a video, several networks bid
  N-->>P: Plays the winning video
  P->>N: Watches to the end
  N->>A: Signed server confirmation, with payout
  A->>A: Check signature, new ID, matches our request
  A->>L: Add credits worth about 70% of the payout
  L-->>P: Credits appear, pill bumps

What's here

  • Rewards follow what each video actually paid.
  • Several networks bid, so later videos still pay well.
  • Suspected bots never get ads, which protects the ad account.

Adjust here

  • The share of each payout that becomes credits (70% today).
  • Videos allowed per hour and per day.
4

Long builds and the cost check

Before a big job starts, the agent writes a plan and Wanlly estimates what it will cost, from the plan's size and from what similar past builds actually used. The person sees the estimate against their balance and decides. If credits run out mid-build anyway, the work pauses and is saved, never lost.

How the estimate works

  • The plan's size is counted exactly with Anthropic's token-counting tool.
  • Past builds of the same kind give a realistic range, which gets more accurate with every build.
  • Shown as a range, never a promise.

Why nothing is lost

  • Each session has a hard budget equal to the person's credits.
  • At the budget the agent pauses with files kept; work is saved at every finished milestone.
  • Raising the budget resumes it where it stopped.

Adjust here

  • When the warning appears (estimate above 80% of credits?).
  • When the mid-build heads-up fires (80% used?).
  • Whether "Start anyway" is allowed for first-time users.
5

Money and runway

Ads pay monthly, a month late. AI must be paid as it's used. A float covers the gap, auto-reload keeps credits topped up, and a budget controller scales usage down gently if the float runs low.

flowchart LR
  ADNET["Ad networks
pay monthly, about 30 days late"] --> BANK["Business USD account"]
  START["Your launch budget
and startup credits"] --> BANK
  BANK --> CARD["USD business card"]
  CARD --> RELOAD["Anthropic auto-reload
tops up when credits run low"]
  RELOAD --> AI["AI usage"]
  STARTUP["AWS or Google credits"] --> BACKUP["Claude on Bedrock or Vertex
backup route"]
  BACKUP --> AI
  GOV["Budget controller
float ÷ days to next payout"] --> AI
  GOV --> ALERT["Alerts
low float, failed reload, card expiry"]
  BANK --> TAX["Set aside for tax"]

What's here

  • Payments are automatic once set up.
  • Low float means gentler limits, never an outage.
  • A backup route covers billing failures.

Adjust here

  • Float size (60 days of AI spend suggested).
  • Global daily safety cap.
  • Tax set-aside percentage.
6

Abuse defences

Each layer assumes the one before it can fail. Automatic actions go up to a freeze; bans always get a person's review.

flowchart TD
  IN["Sign-up or request"] --> L1["Cloudflare firewall and Turnstile"]
  L1 --> L2["Verified email and phone
device fingerprint, country checks"]
  L2 --> L3["Server-only money
reserve first, caps at every level"]
  L3 --> LOG["Every request and ad event logged"]
  LOG --> SCORE["Risk score every 5 minutes"]
  SCORE --> A1["Watch"]
  SCORE --> A2["Slow down"]
  SCORE --> A3["Challenge again"]
  SCORE --> A4["Freeze and alert you"]
  A4 --> HUMAN["You review in admin"]
  HUMAN --> BAN["Ban, reverse fake credits"]
  HUMAN --> OK["Unfreeze"]
