AI products

  1. Writing · Vision

    TumaCV

    Tailored CVs and cover letters for Kenyan job seekers. Paste a job, get an application written for it, and pay per application with M-Pesa, Airtel Money or card.

    Live
    tumacv.vercel.app ↗

    What the AI does

    • Rewrites your CV and drafts a cover letter for each specific job posting.
    • Reads a job advert from a screenshot, so you don't have to retype it.
    • Writes two industry news articles a day and a personal insight on your dashboard.
    • Runs its own marketing: writes posts for X, LinkedIn and Facebook, plus win-back emails, then sends them by email, WhatsApp and SMS.

    Runs on

    GeminiNext.jsSupabaseIntaSendResendVercel

    One shared AI caller falls back to the next model when one is retired or rate-limited.

  2. Writing · Vision

    Wanlly

    A free AI workspace with four tools: Chat, Code, Design and Images. They share one composer. You earn credits by choosing to watch short sponsor spots, so nobody is locked out for lack of a card.

    Prototype · build plan done

    What the AI does

    • Chats on a fast default model, with a stronger model unlocked using credits.
    • Code mode works on your GitHub repos through a GitHub App and opens pull requests.
    • Generates images and design previews in the same flow: prompt, working card, result.
    • Charges from the real token count the model reports, and refunds when a call fails.

    Runs on

    Claude Haiku 5.5Claude Sonnet 5.5CloudflareNeonClerkR2

    Unit cost: about $0.0006 per chat message, so $5 covers roughly 8,000 free messages.

  3. Writing · Vision

    Reachly

    Verified creator profiles. A creator connects their accounts and gets one public page a brand can trust, because every number says where it came from.

    In build

    What the AI does

    • Reads an uploaded insights screenshot and turns it into numbers the creator reviews before saving.
    • Profiles the creator: category, content pillars, audience, tone, languages and brand safety, with a confidence level and its reasoning.
    • Cross-checks follower counts against the YouTube Data API.

    Runs on

    Gemini Flash-LiteYouTube APINext.jsSupabase

    Every figure is tagged Verified (from the platform), Self-reported (from a screenshot) or Claimed (typed in).

  4. Speech · Vision

    SokoOS

    The operating system for Kenyan businesses that sell on Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp. It covers orders, M-Pesa payments, riders, stock and a ledger that balances itself. Installable and works offline.

    In build

    What the AI does

    • Smart Capture: say a sale out loud or point the camera at a receipt, and it books the transaction.
    • Decides credit or debit from the words you used, and shows its reasoning.
    • Imports an M-Pesa or bank statement and skips anything already booked, matched by transaction code.
    • Your CFO: a plain brief on what the business kept, where money went, and what needs attention, with the working behind every figure.

    Runs on

    On-device speechNext.js PWATypeScript

    Understands how sellers talk: “three thousand five hundred from Grace”, “packaging for 2k cash”, “paid the rider 350 bob”.

  5. Engine

    Chess Arena

    Paid bracket chess tournaments. Register, get seeded into a live bracket, play on a server-enforced clock, and the winner takes the prize pool.

    In build

    What the AI does

    • Runs every finished game through Stockfish and flags games that look engine-assisted.
    • Sends flagged games to an admin for review instead of banning anyone automatically.
    • Validates every move on the server, so a client can't forge a move or a result.

    Runs on

    StockfishNext.jsSupabaseIntaSend

    A drawn knockout match never stalls the bracket: it spawns a tiebreak with colours swapped and the clock halved.

About

Alongside them I build ad-ops tools that turn hours of reporting and screenshotting into a single click. I keep them on free tiers until they earn their keep.
