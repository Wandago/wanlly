## Lobby

Amina creates a room and pastes the link `…/QBRF` into the group WhatsApp. Everyone joins with a name. Amina picks *The Treasurer of Mwangaza Court* ("Content notes: a fatal fall in a stairwell, not described graphically; debt and money worries in a savings group"), leaves the version on *Surprise me*, and presses **Start**. The server picks version A and deals the cards. Nobody's browser receives anyone else's card.

## Briefing (90 s)

Everyone sees the same case file: the rooftop harambee, Grace leaving at 21:10, found at 21:35 on the back-stairwell landing, *alive at 21:15 and fallen by 21:25*, and the cast list with who plays whom.

Each player also sees a private card:

- **Amina (Brian, Culprit)** reads, in a red box: *"At 21:13 you followed Grace into the back stairwell… you pushed her… went down the back stairs to the parking yard (arriving at 21:24)…"* Her cover story: *"I went down to my car at 21:12 to fetch an extension cable…"* Danger: *"Joseph sometimes checks the parking yard. The live stream may show which door you left by. And you never did bring the cable up."* Ability: one quiet search.
- **Otieno (Wambui, Detective):** two searches per round, verified. Secret: a UK nursing job she hasn't announced. Knowledge: *"I was on the mic from about 21:05 until 21:36… Faith was dancing by the speakers the whole time."*
- **Njeri (Kevin):** the secret about the cigarettes and the KSh 2,000. Knowledge: *"I was at Mama Pendo's kiosk… from just after 21:05 until about 21:28."*
- **Hassan (Faith):** the KSh 40,000 chama debt and the shylock loan. Knowledge: Grace's 19:40 text, *"only announcing totals, not names"*.
- **Chebet (Daniel, Insider):** the tanker "tips". Knowledge: *"From 21:14 to 21:26 I was at the gate, helping the tent-hire driver…"* Ability: can search Flat 4B in round 1.

Everyone presses Ready, and the phase ends early.

## Discovery (60 s)

The board fills with the three starting clues: **Grace's last message, 21:15** (sent from Flat 4B), **Found at 21:35** (Kevin found her; Wambui reached her at 21:37), and **The neighbour in 5A** (a cry and a clatter at 21:19). Because nobody plays Joseph, his statement is posted as a *statement to the police*: *"At 21:21 a car alarm chirped, so I walked into the parking yard with my torch. Nobody was in the yard…"*

Amina, as Brian, gets her story in early and shares her cover as a statement: *"I went down to my car at 21:12 to fetch an extension cable…"* On the board, it looks exactly like an innocent statement.

Njeri shares Kevin's kiosk statement. Chat:

> **Hassan:** wait, Brian says he was in the parking from 21:12. Joseph says nobody was there at 21:21?
> **Amina:** I was round the back of the cars, the yard is dark. He didn't see me 🤷🏾‍♀️
> **Otieno:** noted. Searching the gate first.

## Investigation, round 2 (4 min)

The round opens, and everything unshared is published:
- **Grace's purse**, found by Chebet (handed in at round end)
- **Receipt book on the landing**, found by Hassan (handed in at round end)
- **Brian's car**, *handed in anonymously*

Flat 4B is now open to all. Chebet searches it again and finds **Grace's ledger and sticky note**: *"Borehole fund KSh 186,000 moved… to a personal line ending …4471. That is B's line. Confront B before the AGM."* Chebet shares it at once. Njeri searches the stairwell and finds **Cigarette ends on the stairs**. Hassan tries the gate: *Nothing left to find there* (the search isn't used up).

With the defaulters list now public and eyes turning to Faith, Hassan **confesses**: *"I owe the chama KSh 40,000 and took a loan from a shylock…"* He gives up his secret bonus, but the live stream already clears him, so it costs him nothing else.

## What this round shows

- The culprit's quiet search delayed the cable clue by one round, but couldn't bury it.
- Innocent secrets (Kevin's money, Faith's debt) created real suspicion, and both were resolved by evidence, not by confession alone.
- The decisive deduction was about time and place: an alibi ("I was at my car") broken by a "nobody was there" fact. That is exactly what the checker guarantees is available.
