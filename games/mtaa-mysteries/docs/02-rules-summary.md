# 2. Rules on one page

**Players:** 4–6, in a browser (phone or computer). **Time:** about 15–20 minutes per case. **Voice chat:** optional. Text chat is built in.

## The setup

The host creates a room and shares a 4-letter code. Everyone joins with a name, the host picks a case and presses Start, and the game deals each player a character.

**Your private card** tells you who you are, your **role**, your **goal**, a **secret**, and **what you know**. Nobody else can see it.

One player is the **culprit**. Their card says what really happened and gives them a cover story. Everyone else is innocent.

## The one rule

> **Only the culprit may lie.** Everyone else may dodge questions, refuse to answer and keep their secret, but must never say anything false.

Every innocent character has a secret that makes them look suspicious. That is by design.

## How a round goes

| Phase | Time* | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **Briefing** | 1:30 | Read your card. |
| **Discovery** | 1:00 | The case opens: the victim, the time window (for example 21:15–21:25) and where it happened. Starting clues go on the shared board. You may share your character's statements. |
| **Investigation 1** | 4:00 | Search **one** place. What you find is private until you share it. Anything unshared goes on the board, under your name, when the round ends. |
| **Investigation 2** | 4:00 | One more search each. Restricted places open to everyone. |
| **Closing arguments** | 2:00 | Accuse one suspect, citing a clue, in 280 characters. |
| **The vote** | 1:00 | Vote in secret for the culprit (or abstain). All votes are revealed together. |
| **The truth** | – | The solution, everyone's cards, and the scores. |

\*Standard pace. The host can choose Quick (×0.75) or Relaxed (×1.5), and add one extra minute per phase. When every player presses Ready, the phase ends early.

## Roles

- **Culprit:** may lie. Has one *quiet search* that isn't announced.
- **Detective:** two searches per round. Their clues go straight to the board, marked Verified.
- **Witness:** saw something. Can *recall* one extra detail, once.
- **Insider:** has keys, so can search the restricted place in round 1.
- **Secret-keeper:** no ability; their secret looks bad.

Anyone can **confess** their secret. It clears the air but costs that player the secret bonus.

## How to solve it

For each suspect, ask: *is there evidence putting them somewhere else for the whole window, too far away to reach the scene?* Each case lists travel times. A receipt at a kiosk six minutes away at 21:17 rules someone out of a stairwell at 21:19. The culprit is the one person the evidence can't place elsewhere, and their cover story will clash with a clue.

## Winning

- The culprit is **caught** only with the **most votes outright**. A tie lets them escape.
- **Innocents:** +3 if the group catches the culprit, +2 for voting for them, +1 for citing a decisive clue, and +1 for keeping your secret without being the top suspect.
- **Culprit:** +5 for escaping, +1 for each innocent who voted for another innocent (up to +3).
- Prefer no scores? Turn scoring off in the lobby.
